Source and software evaluation

  • data.nasa.gov is a metadata catalog linking to NASA data archives; OSDR is the suitable source for these biological studies.
  • api.nasa.gov documents OSDR metadata, file, and search access. Unrelated astronomy and imagery APIs were intentionally not integrated.
  • OSDR hosts omics, physiological, phenotypic, and related space-relevant research data.
  • NASA Code and NASA's GeneLab processing repository were evaluated. GeneLab pipelines use NASA Open Source Agreement 1.3, but are specialized omics workflows and unsuitable for this UI; no NASA software was copied or bundled.

Coverage limitations

The reviewed catalog includes human serum/cardiovascular studies, human cell models, and mouse biological-effects research. We did not find a verified OSDR record suitable as a direct live human heart-rate, SpO₂, temperature, or sleep stream. Small cohorts, model organisms, cell cultures, different missions, and assay-specific protocols prevent direct clinical or operational generalization.
